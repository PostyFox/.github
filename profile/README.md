# PostyFox

## What is this project?

Initially giving the capability for people to author posts in PostyBirb and schedule them for posting at a later date (offloaded into the cloud).

## Why not do this as part of the main PostyBirb project?

PostyBirb is about to kick off the v4 rebuild, and is already a massive project. There is not much dev resource there as it is, and not much appetite to push on with a web based version - partly around the aspect of being concerned around security and cost, but also the need to have a lot of ongoing maintenance for it. Doing this as a seperate project allows it to be ran in tandom, giving the best of both worlds.

## Why initially build with a tie on Azure? I hate Microsoft / Google / an-other-cloud-platform!

Azure makes it very easy to kick start a cloud based project, and I am a Microsoft Partner. This gives me a very low bar to getting something in place, including financially, meaning something can be deployed and usable very quickly - with minimal risk. By offloading storage of sensitive things into Microsoft controlled elements, we are making use of industry controlled, stable and secure platforms that *many* organisations already trust around the world, without needing to completely re-engineer the wheel, and can focuss on where the effort is best spent - building features that are actually adding value (and not adding security holes that will leak your data). 

## What are the future plan?

Many. Take a look at the Trello project plan for the latest, but ultimately the goal is to maintain the ability to keep extending what people can do with PostyBirb but *also* add more capabilities that allow people to post from any device, at any time.
